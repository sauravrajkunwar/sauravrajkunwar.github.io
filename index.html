Saurav Raj Kunwar

I am Saurav Raj Kunwar, an applied economist and Postdoctoral Researcher at Texas Tech University.

My work focuses on productivity, firm and producer performance, incentives, innovation, resource allocation, and decision-making under uncertainty. I use applied econometrics, causal inference, structural modeling, simulation, and spatial data to study how economic agents respond to changing technologies, policies, markets, and external conditions.

My research spans firm productivity, technological change, agricultural and environmental applications, climate and weather risk, and policy-relevant economic measurement. I completed my PhD in Agricultural and Applied Economics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and I also hold an MS in Agricultural and Applied Economics from the University of Georgia.

What I Work On

Productivity, Firms, and Economic Performance

I study how incentives, technologies, market conditions, and external shocks shape productivity, efficiency, and performance across firms and producers.

Policy, Incentives, and Decision-Making

I evaluate how policies, information, and resource constraints affect economic choices, trade-offs, and outcomes under uncertainty.

Econometrics, Structural Modeling, and Data

I use causal inference, structural and productivity models, simulation, spatial data, and reproducible workflows in R, Stata, Python, GAMS, and related tools.
